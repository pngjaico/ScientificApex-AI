## Proposed question and estimands

Primary question: what frequency of PSG-detected OSA is observed after secondary VPI surgery in people with cleft palate, accounting for procedure, diagnostic definition and follow-up?

- Primary estimand: OSA proportion among participants actually assessed by PSG in an eligible surgical cohort, with explicit horizon and threshold. Also report PSG coverage of the eligible population.
- De novo incidence: a separate estimand requiring documented absence of baseline OSA and follow-up.
- Comparative effects: separate estimands requiring within-study comparisons and appropriate assessment of design/confounding.
- Ascertainment-source differences: contextual descriptions. Ratios of proportions from different studies do not estimate sensitivity, multiplicative underascertainment or mechanisms of underrecognition.

These repair proposals are informed by knowledge of previous results. They are not original prespecification. The submitted PROSPERO protocol must determine which are retained methods, clarifications or post-hoc changes.

## Synthesis decision

1. Do not predetermine that NMA or proportion meta-analysis is mandatory. The blanket absence-of-randomized-trials claim was incorrect; trial existence alone does not establish a valid network.
2. Assess clinical compatibility, definitions, horizon, selection for PSG and cohort independence before pooling. Distinguish studies, arms and age strata.
3. With one study per procedure, report that study's estimate. Do not estimate between-study heterogeneity from its age strata or use between-technique Q as proof of superiority.
4. If independent compatible studies exist, justify models and intervals before the repaired synthesis. Distinguish study-proportion, single-arm and synthesis intervals. Wilson is a score interval; Clopper-Pearson is exact. Do not conflate their labels.
5. If compatibility or independence is inadequate, retain structured descriptive synthesis. Assess suitable comparative evidence separately; consider NMA only after demonstrating connectivity and plausible transitivity.
6. Define repeated-time and overlapping-sample rules before reanalysis. Report study-specific times rather than assigning a uniform 6 to 12 month window.
