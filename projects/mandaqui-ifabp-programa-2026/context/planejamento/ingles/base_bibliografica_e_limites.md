## Limits and corrections

Preliminary searches used PubMed, publication/PMC pages and an attempt to read NCT06212921. ClinicalTrials.gov HTML did not provide readable status/results; no claim of open or closed recruitment is made. Web access to the Nuzzo full text returned a browser check; the synthesis used its abstract, not full text.

Coordinator verification discarded two candidate IDs: PMID 28575389 is a urinary infection antibiotic trial, not the I-FABP timing study; the correct ID is 28630622. PMID 34518541 concerns breast cancer, not Nuzzo; the correct ID is 34556697. A subagent note contained the first error, corrected before this delivery. Animal/neonatal studies and a retracted result in the broad search were not used to support adult performance.

The internal GastroSP NMA scaffold describes an exploratory ranking favouring urine, with a sparse network and imprecision. I did not confirm a DOI, IJCR publication or award document. The user's account is retained as context without treating an award as proof of clinical validity.

The C: Life Science planner selected an inappropriate microbiome route through the word “intestinal” and reported a missing plugin. Its execution was not simulated. Targeted retrieval used PubMed and primary sources. No new formal scientific corpus was started in D:; portfolio studies remain protocol candidates requiring final registration and a specific search.
