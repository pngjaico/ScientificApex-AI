### Structured Mini-Abstract (for Table of Contents, strictly <= 50 words)
In 10,165 patients with hepatocellular carcinoma from the NCI SEER registry, explainable machine learning with clinical monotonic constraints and conformal prediction significantly outperformed Milan criteria in predicting 5-year cancer-specific survival (AUROC 0.6773 vs 0.5000, p < 0.001), providing calibrated uncertainty bounds and superior clinical net benefit for curative surgical allocation.
