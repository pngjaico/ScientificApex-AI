Diagnosis of all ischemia remains NO-GO without a defensible reference. Death before resection requires competing-event analysis, not classification as a healthy control.

4. **Dissertation:** matrix comparison and incremental performance in the same database are not independent replications. The master's prioritizes analytical validity/applicability with its own contrast; fix the matrix before outcome analyses. Results may require an integrated article.
5. **Portfolio:** candidates are not promised papers. P/C/I classification and explicit integration prevent fragmentation; external validation and new populations require their own data.

This applies the critique to planning; it is not independent approval of the final protocol. Flow, events, assay, budget, team and eligibility are not yet demonstrated.
