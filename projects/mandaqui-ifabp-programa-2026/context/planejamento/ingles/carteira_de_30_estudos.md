## Independence gates

Before promoting each candidate: its own question/estimand; sufficient information; contribution beyond the other manuscripts; declared overlap; compatible cost/timeline; applicable protocol and registration; appropriate ethics and consent. Otherwise, integrate or discard. Do not repeat main tables with a different author order.

**Recommended first batch:** C02 with C03/C16/C17/C20 integrated; C05 with C06/C07 integrated; C04 if it has substance of its own; C08; main doctoral result; C22 if a partner is available. C01, C13, and C27 merit separate design, but are not certain publications. Final selection depends on the data and contribution, not an artificial quantity target.

Do not turn “mortality,” “length of stay,” “sex,” “age,” “a new cutoff,” and “one biomarker at a time” into separate articles from the same sample by default. Those analyses belong to the central question unless justified by distinct methods and genuinely distinct information.
