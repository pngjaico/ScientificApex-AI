## C — Systematic review, PRISMA, RoB/GRADE and meta-analysis

Use meta-analise-medica. Register/freeze protocol before screening; preserve reproducible searches, deduplication, dual screening and independent extraction. Distinguish studies from reports; reconcile PRISMA counts and exclusion reasons. Choose design-appropriate RoB and outcome-specific GRADE with justifications. ## D — Clinical dataset, baseline, ML/DL and article

Compare simple baselines; report AUROC/AUPRC, prevalence, calibration, intervals and justified utility. DL must fit the question/modality and be compared with baseline, ablations and error analysis.
