## Explicit limits

This is an operationally improved scientific workspace, **not scientific release
certification**. No comprehensive scientific acceptance benchmark, remote CI,
router macro-F1 study, graph gold-query performance matrix, or comparative model
efficiency study was completed. Some changed critical/legacy modules remain below
90% coverage (including evidence, project lifecycle and browser operations).
The governance project is not promoted to accepted/released by this report.

Plugin availability does not establish authentication or successful live calls.
The Life Science Research package has multiple candidates and remains blocked
until an explicit binding is chosen. Consensus and Scite are optional pilot
ideas, not installed requirements. Human scientific review remains essential:
hashes establish identity/freshness of inputs, not semantic truth, reviewer
identity, clinical validity or correctness of a methodological judgment.
