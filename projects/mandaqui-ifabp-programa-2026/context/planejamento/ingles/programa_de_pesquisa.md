## Flavio's doctoral thesis

**Working title:** “Additional prognostic value of I-FABP beyond clinical and CT assessment in small bowel obstruction: prospective validation and applicability at Mandaqui.”

**Primary question:** In adults with SBO assessed by CT and without resection at enrollment, does the biomarker add information about the risk of **resection for histologically confirmed intestinal necrosis within 30 days**, compared with the same clinical and CT assessment without the biomarker?

This target is an event observed under usual care. **It is not equivalent to detecting all ischemia, early mucosal injury, or the biological need for surgery.** Clinical CT influences who undergoes surgery; absence of surgery does not prove absence of ischemia. For the prognostic question, patients without resection have absence of the observed surgical event, not “a proven normal intestine.” Death before resection is a competing event and requires an explicit approach; it will not be treated as a healthy control or a simple non-informative loss. Histology unavailability will be classified, audited, and included in sensitivity analyses, without silently converting it to a negative case.

Time zero, the ordering of collection, CT, report, and treatment decision, the target population, and the 30-day window must be finalized in the protocol. Do not use information collected after the event as a baseline predictor. If the objective becomes diagnosis of ischemia, a different estimand and defensible reference standard will be needed; do not switch labels in the manuscript.

**Proposed architecture:** consecutive enrollment, first clinical/CT assessment as comparator, collection without delaying care, and biomarker blinded to the clinical team. Define one episode per patient in the primary analysis or model dependence from re-enrollments. Record eligible patients not enrolled, contraindicated contrast, lack of urine/anuria, times, and losses. The population with contrast-enhanced CT does not automatically represent all patients with obstruction.

Urine is a candidate primary matrix based on the hypothesis derived from the internal NMA, **not on established clinical superiority**. Fix the matrix/scale after checking analytical validity and before accessing outcome associations. Paired plasma enables methodological comparison. Do not select the best matrix and threshold based on results and present them as independent confirmation in the same dataset.

Compare a clinically justifiable, frozen base model with the same model plus one biomarker, only if there are enough events for its degrees of freedom. Report calibration, discrimination, and uncertainty; internal validation does not create events. Seek a second center for external validation. A temporal cohort at Mandaqui is temporal validation, not external validation. A batch assay may evaluate prognosis, but does not demonstrate benefit in real decision-making or availability in the emergency department.

**Doctoral scope:** incremental validity + planned independent validation + applicability. The program is not justified by the number of kits, subgroups, or papers. Without adequate workflow/events and platform, narrow the question, add centers, or do not proceed.

## The user's master's dissertation

**Working title:** “Analytical validity and applicability of I-FABP in urine and plasma in the emergency department: a paired study in patients with small bowel obstruction.”

**Independent question:** Which matrix yields analytically valid and operationally usable measurements under defined collection, transport, and storage conditions?

Propose **one paired primary contrast**: proportion of valid results per eligible patient in urine versus plasma, counting missing samples and analytical failure according to distinct definitions. The denominator must be explicit: applicability among all eligible patients and analytical performance in samples actually obtained are not the same rate. Plan precision of the difference and dependence between matrices, without using the clinical-event N to calculate this objective.

Validation of precision, stability, interferents, and quantification range must follow the platform actually contracted. Aliquots may test pre-analytical conditions without adding care procedures, if covered by consent/ethics. Technical replicates do not increase patient N. Record absolute urine concentration, urinary creatinine, renal function, urine output, AKI, and dialysis, without presuming that a ratio “corrects” all renal influence. Clinical discrimination and interaction analyses are secondary and will be confirmatory only if powered and registered as such.

This allows the master's degree to be completed before all thesis events have matured. If the work becomes just an additional blood-versus-urine table, it should be integrated into the main article, not fragmented. Two theses may yield one scientifically stronger joint article.

## Sample, budget, and governance

**Do not set 120 patients for convenience.** Information is limited by events and useful denominators, not by the number of papers. The matrix comparison has its own paired calculation; the prognostic model requires calculation/simulation with documented prevalence, complexity, calibration, and losses. A purely illustrative sensitivity-precision example would require about 62 positive cases for an assumed sensitivity of 0.80 and approximate half-width of 0.10; this does not size the thesis model. If the event is rare, the total required may be much greater than 120. No local prevalence has been measured in this package.

Build the platform budget as: patients × matrices × time points × replicates, plus calibrators, controls, failures, storage, personnel, and follow-up. “Thirty ideas” do not fund thirty panels or thirty collection time points. Cost remains unknown until quotations are obtained. The grant/aid, doctoral scholarship, and master's scholarship have their own eligibility rules and budget categories.

Register an umbrella protocol with modules and amendment rules, while respecting when each study requires its own registration. Do not open thirty empty registrations. Maintain an overlap matrix, dictionary, cohort/periods, authorship by contribution, cross-citations, and analysis version. A sex analysis, a threshold, or a null result does not automatically justify a separate article. Do not make the master's degree conditional on a positive thesis result.
